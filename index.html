How an order runs

From requirement to dispatch

Each stage is confirmed before the next one starts, so you know exactly what is being produced before the bulk run begins.

Requirement and quotation

You send the specification, quantity and destination. We quote against it, line by line.

Sample and approval

A pre-production sample is made and approved in writing. That sample becomes the reference for the order.

Production and checks

Bulk production runs against the approved sample, with checks against the specification before packing.

Packing and dispatch

Retail and export packing, carton marking, documentation and a container loading plan.

Custom and OEM

Your brand, made to your brief

A large part of our work leaves the factory under someone else's name. Private-label and OEM programmes run from artwork through to retail-ready packaging.
