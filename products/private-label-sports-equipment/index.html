Private-label production lets a distributor, retailer or new brand sell under its own name without running a factory. Divine Cuts manufactures the product, applies the buyer's branding and packs it retail-ready for the destination market.

A private-label programme usually starts with one or two lines, then widens into a range once the first shipment sells through.

Features

  • Retail-ready packaging for your market
  • Consistent good–better–best range structure
