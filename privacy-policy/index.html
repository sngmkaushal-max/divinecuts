Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Divine Cuts handles personal information when you visit thedivineexport.com or contact us through this website. Divine Cuts is based in Punjab, India.

Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form may ask for your name, company, buyer type, country, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, product requirement, quantity, customisation requirements and message. Please provide only information relevant to your business enquiry.

How website enquiries are sent

The enquiry form does not submit information to a Divine Cuts website database or website server. It prepares a message and opens your chosen email application or WhatsApp. You must review and send that message yourself. Until you press send in that external application, the enquiry has not been delivered to Divine Cuts.

If you send the message, the information is transmitted through the email or WhatsApp service you use and is received in the relevant Divine Cuts email or WhatsApp account. Those third-party services have their own privacy terms and practices.

How we use enquiry information

We use information you send to respond to your request, assess product and sourcing requirements, prepare quotations, discuss samples and specifications, communicate about potential or confirmed orders, and maintain relevant business correspondence. We do not sell or rent enquiry information.

Campaign and referral information

Where campaign parameters such as UTM source, medium or campaign are present in the page address, the enquiry form may include those values, the enquiry page address and the referring page in the message it prepares. These details are sent to us only if you choose to send the prepared message.

Storage and retention

The website itself does not retain submitted enquiry records. Messages that you send may remain in our business email or WhatsApp accounts and related backups. We keep business correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage customer and supplier relationships, handle orders, meet applicable legal or accounting requirements, and resolve disputes. We may retain records longer where the law requires it.

Cookies, analytics and third-party resources

As of the date above, the website code in our repository does not contain a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID, Google Tag Manager container ID or Google Analytics tag. We therefore do not currently claim to collect GA4 analytics through this website. If analytics or advertising tools are configured later, this policy should be updated to describe the tools, data and choices involved before they are activated.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google infrastructure to retrieve font files, which can involve technical information such as your IP address being processed by that provider. The site also links to external services such as WhatsApp, email providers and social networks. Their privacy policies govern your use of those services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled through email, messaging, hosting or other service providers needed to operate our communications and website, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights. We do not currently operate a website enquiry database.

Your requests and contact

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent to us, request correction where appropriate, or ask us to delete information where we are not required or otherwise entitled to retain it. We may need to verify your identity and consider applicable legal, contractual and record-keeping obligations before acting.

Privacy contact: info@thedivineexport.com

Applicable law and updates

This notice is prepared with reference to applicable laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules and notifications made under it, to the extent and from the dates those provisions apply. The Act and Rules have phased commencement dates. This notice is not a certification that every statutory obligation has already commenced or that the business has completed every compliance step. We may update this policy when our practices or applicable law change.
