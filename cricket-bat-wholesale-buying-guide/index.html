Prepare a clear bulk enquiry for cricket bats by defining willow grade, profile, weight, handle, finishing and export requirements.

1. Willow type and grade

Specify the willow type and grade you require. Ask for photographs and written specifications for the exact model, since grade labels and appearance can differ between suppliers.

2. Weight and profile

Record your desired finished weight, blade profile, edge and spine characteristics, balance or pickup preference and target playing level. Use a reference image or approved sample when possible.

3. Handle and finishing

Confirm handle type, grip, toe protection, finish and optional features. Ask what is included in the quoted configuration.

4. Private label branding

Ask about sticker artwork, logo placement, toe branding, retail packaging and feasibility of custom details. Approve a sample before bulk production.

5. Sample approval and inspection

Discuss sample availability and charges, courier costs and approval criteria. Check finish, dimensions, weight and balance before confirming the bulk reference.

6. Packing and export

Confirm protection, carton configuration, shipment quantities, destination, delivery terms and documents needed by your importing business.
