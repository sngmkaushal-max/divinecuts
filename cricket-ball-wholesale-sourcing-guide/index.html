Cricket Ball Wholesale Sourcing Guide for Buyers

Retailers, distributors, clubs and academies should define ball type and construction before requesting a bulk quotation.

Retailers, distributors, clubs and academies should define ball type and construction before requesting a bulk quotation.

1. Intended use

Clarify whether the balls are for training, recreational play, club use or match use. Intended use determines which specifications buyers should compare.

2. Construction and material

Ask suppliers to state construction, outer material, seam details, core or filling, finish and other relevant specifications. Similar-looking products may differ in performance.

3. Size, weight and standards

Specify size and weight range and any playing-standard requirement. If a standard is required, name it and request confirmation for the exact product.

4. Sample and quality checks

Request a sample when possible. Define approval checks such as size, weight, seam, finish, consistency and bounce or other use-appropriate performance checks.

5. Branding and packing

Discuss logo printing, colour, retail packaging, labels and carton marks. Confirm feasibility, quantities and sample approval.

6. Export quotation brief

Send the product type, specification, quantity, packaging, destination country, delivery preference and required documents so the supplier can assess sourcing and shipping options.
